What this Draft uses

SHOPIFY

Activity & traceability

Order ID/name, created date, customer/recipient, tags, note/custom attributes, discounts, financial and fulfillment status, line items, SKU, quantity, and order/item values.

QUICKBOOKS

Accounting evidence

Purchase, bill, or expense transaction date, vendor/entity, transaction reference, total amount, expense/account lines, item/account references, and memo when available.

DERIVED / REVIEW

Tracker-only fields

Sponsorship type, detection reason, confidence, accounting match status, and review note. These are derived or reviewed — never silently invented.

Detection logic for Draft

Keywords are checked against Shopify tags, notes, custom attributes, discount labels/codes, and relevant product/customer metadata. Every record preserves the field that triggered classification.

Intentionally not assumed yet

This draft does not assume ROI, ROAS, CAC, influencer revenue attribution, social engagement, or campaign budget metrics, because those were not confirmed for this project.
