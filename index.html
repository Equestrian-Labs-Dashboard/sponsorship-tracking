What this Draft uses

SHOPIFY

Activity & traceability

Order ID/name, created date, customer/recipient, tags, note/custom attributes, discounts, financial and fulfillment status, line items, SKU, quantity and order/item values.

QUICKBOOKS

Accounting evidence

Purchase/bill/expense transaction date, vendor/entity, transaction reference, total amount, expense/account lines, item/account references and memo when available.

DERIVED / REVIEW

Tracker-only fields

Sponsorship type, detection reason, confidence, accounting match status and review note. These are derived from source data or explicitly reviewed — never silently invented.

Detection logic for Draft

Keywords are checked against available Shopify tags, note/custom attributes, discount labels/codes and relevant product/customer metadata. A record stores the exact field that triggered classification. Low-confidence or conflicting records go to Review Queue.
